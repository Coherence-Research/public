# Code of Conduct

This repository hosts a formal specification and the community materials around it. Participation is open to anyone. The conduct expectations are short because the standard is simple: engage with the work, not the person.

## Expected

- Argue structurally. Challenges, counterexamples, and criticism of the specification are welcome — that is what the repository is for. Direct them at claims, derivations, and documents.
- Assume competence. Contributors and questioners arrive at different depths; a question that looks basic often isn't.
- Keep discussions on the record. Technical disagreements are resolved in issues and Discussions, where the reasoning stays inspectable.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- Bad-faith participation: sealioning, derailing, spam, or promotional flooding.
- Misrepresenting the canonical documents — quoting altered text as canonical, or presenting modified versions as the specification.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove content and restrict participation for conduct violations, independent of the technical merit of the contribution. Judgment calls are the maintainers'; the reasoning will be stated.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues via [coherenceresearch.com](https://coherenceresearch.com) rather than in public threads.
